### ⚡ Executive Brief

Undergraduate researcher at the **Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (B.Tech '28)** majoring in Mechanical Engineering with a **Minor in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (CMInDS)**.

Working at the nexus of **stochastic modeling, quantitative alpha generation, and ultra-low-latency real-time autonomous vehicle control systems**:
- 📈 **Master Consultant @ WorldQuant BRAIN**: Ranked **#42 globally** (out of 5,700+) in the Power Pool Alpha Competition (11th in India). Selected as **1 of 12 consultants worldwide** for the **Global IQC '25 Finals** in Singapore. Maintained an out-of-sample after-cost Information Ratio of **2.39** across 320+ orthogonal alphas.
- 🏎️ **Team Manager & Controls @ SeDriCa (UMIC)**: Spearheading controls and state estimation for India's premier Level 5 autonomous driving initiative under Prof. Archak Mittal. Developing a **100 Hz LibTorch-Acados HyperMPC** runtime and Joint EKF state estimation with RTK GNSS, while managing a 30+ engineer cross-functional team and an annual budget of INR 4M+.
- 📑 **Published Researcher**: Author of *[Mathematics of Derivative Pricing: Theory and Computational Implementation](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=10485196)* (SSRN Pre-print ID: 6439720).
